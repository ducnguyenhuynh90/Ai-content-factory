# The Night FedEx Went to Vegas
_Founder Stories · No. 1 · 623 words · ~4.46 min · 16:9_

## Learning goals
- Explain why a startup with a good idea can still die: cash, not the idea, sets the deadline
- Tell the difference between buying time and building a business
- Recognise network ideas that only work at full size, and why they need long funding

## Principles
1. **Cash is oxygen** — A startup doesn't die when its idea is bad. It dies when the money runs out.
2. **A gamble buys time, not a business** — Luck can buy a week. Only people who believe in the plan buy years.
3. **Some ideas only work at full size** — Half a network is worth almost nothing, so plan to survive the expensive years.
