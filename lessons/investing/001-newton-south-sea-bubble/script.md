# The Genius and the Bubble
_Lessons from Market History · No. 1 · 617 words · ~4.41 min · 4:3_

## Learning goals
- Explain the difference between price and value with a real historical example
- Recognise how watching others profit can override your own analysis
- Describe Benjamin Graham's margin of safety and why rules must be set before the market tests them

## Principles
1. **Price is not value** — When price runs far ahead of what a business can earn, the gap eventually closes.
2. **The crowd is not evidence** — Other people getting rich is a feeling, not a fact about the asset.
3. **Set your rules before the test** — A margin of safety, decided in calm, protects you from yourself in the frenzy.
